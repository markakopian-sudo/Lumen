DMCA Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

1. Our approach

Mark respects the rights of copyright owners and responds to notices of alleged infringement that follow the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

2. What this covers

This policy applies to content that users post on Lumen, such as chat messages, bios, nicknames and profile photos. Lumen can also open and link to other websites. We do not host that content, so please send complaints about it to the operator of that website.

3. Sending a notice of infringement

If you believe content on Lumen infringes your copyright, send our designated agent a written notice that includes all of the following:

  • your physical or electronic signature, or that of a person authorized to act for the copyright owner;
  • a description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed;
  • a description of the material you say is infringing and where it is on Lumen, such as the channel, username and time of a message;
  • your name, mailing address, telephone number and email address;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  • a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act for the owner.

4. What we do

When we receive a complete notice, we will promptly remove or disable access to the material, and we may tell the user who posted it. We may pass the notice, including your contact details, to that user.

5. Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send our designated agent a counter-notice that includes:

  • your physical or electronic signature;
  • a description of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal;
  • a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed because of a mistake or misidentification;
  • your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, if outside the United States, any district where the operator can be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We will send your counter-notice to the person who made the original complaint. Unless that person tells us within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the material.

6. Repeat infringers

In appropriate circumstances, we will end the accounts of users who are repeat infringers.

7. False claims

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly makes a false claim that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages. Please make sure your notice is accurate.

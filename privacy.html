Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains what information Lumen collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Lumen is run by [your name]. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

2. Information you give us

If you create an account, we store:

  • your username, email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store the password itself;
  • optional profile details: nickname, bio and profile photo;
  • your chat messages;
  • your friends, friend requests and friend-request setting;
  • your rank, the codes you have redeemed, and your progress, such as coins, Shop items, settings, watchlist, saved links and high scores.

3. Information stored on your device

Lumen uses your browser’s local storage, not cookies, to remember your settings, open tabs, progress and sign-in. If you use Lumen without the account server, accounts are kept only in your own browser. Clearing your browser’s site data removes this information from your device.

4. Information collected automatically

Lumen does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The services that host and deliver Lumen, such as GitHub, jsDelivr and Cloudflare, may log technical information like your IP address, browser type and the time of a request as part of providing their services.

5. How we use information

We use it to run the Service: signing you in, showing your profile and messages, matching friend requests, applying rewards and codes, preventing abuse, and keeping the Service safe. We do not use it for advertising.

6. What other people can see

Other signed-in users can see your username, nickname, bio, profile photo, rank, name style and badge, your join date, and the messages you post in chat. Your email address, password and friends list are not shown to other users. The person who runs Lumen can access account records, including email addresses, in order to operate and protect the Service.

7. Service providers and other sites

  • Cloudflare runs our account server and storage, and the AI feature.
  • Google Fonts delivers some of the fonts you see, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Other websites you open inside Lumen are separate from us. They may collect information about you, and their own policies apply.

We may share information if the law requires it or if we need to protect people’s safety or the Service.

8. AI feature

Questions you type into the AI section are sent to an AI service run through Cloudflare to produce an answer. We do not intentionally keep them, but please do not enter private or sensitive information.

9. How long we keep information

  • Accounts are kept until you delete them.
  • Each chat channel keeps its most recent messages, and they are deleted automatically after about 7 days.
  • Sign-in sessions last up to 30 days. Announcements last up to 30 days.

When you delete your account, we remove your profile record, email lookup, searchable nickname, friend connections and sign-in. Messages you already posted can stay visible until they expire.

10. Security

We scramble passwords before storing them and use encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and do not reuse it.

11. Your choices

  • Edit your nickname, bio and photo any time from your profile.
  • Turn friend requests off in Settings.
  • Delete your account from Account, then Delete account.
  • Ask us to correct or remove information by contacting us below.

12. Children

Lumen is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
